# Architectural planning method audit — 30 October 2026

The current design-first method is a useful separation of design from operation execution, but it is not a coordinated architectural planning system. It asks the model to select architecture, solve its topology, independently calculate most component coordinates, and certify those choices in prose in the same response. Software predominantly checks the result after those commitments are frozen. **A bounded relationship-first revision is appropriate to investigate; a wholesale layout engine or model switch is not yet justified.** Independent audit should precede that revision.

This is a repository/evidence audit and offline arithmetic experiment. No external AI, network request, database connection, commit or deployment was made. Existing uncommitted work was preserved. The only application correction is the relationship-review budget described below. No retained proposal was repaired, constructed or relabelled successful.

## Root causes supported by the failures

| Finding | Evidence | Interpretation and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Topology is described without an executable access obligation | Terra has four upper suites without entrance anchors and no selected stair/landing/void. Sol names routes and doors, but ground doors lie at Z=4 while their rotated wall openings are at Z=8. | References and route prose do not establish traversable thresholds. Missing route IDs alone do not prove every route is impossible: Sol's upper wing slabs could carry side corridors. |
| Reservations and constructed solids share one representation | Terra's wing “shells” are full solid wall blocks. Sol's stair reserve is a 2.6 × 4 × 6.5 m solid occupying the entry approach. Both defer changes that would replace immutable anchors. | A reservation should constrain later construction, not have to survive as a solid. The current fidelity requirement correctly detects changes, but freezes the wrong abstraction when the proposal chooses a placeholder. |
| Dependent quantities are independently authored | Terra's 11 space boxes use walking Y as centre Y. Sol improves four upper boxes but retains nine bounds/datum mismatches. Rotated opening/door locations disagree; outdoor slab edges leave gaps. | A single selected datum or wall-local opening should generate dependent coordinates once. Solving that arithmetic does not design a room plan. |
| Architectural hierarchy and circulation are not resolved together | Both choose central-hall/mirrored-wing estates. Terra's claims of lower wings conflict with roof heights; Sol's profiled roof intersects parts of side roof solids. Neither has a workable committed stair system. | Section, access and massing need an explicit dependency review before geometric commitments freeze. Plan overlap at different heights, as in Terra, is not itself a roof collision. |
| Capacity and actual suites remain conflated in design output | Six names coexist with shared blocks, incomplete boundary loops, deferred bathrooms/dressing/service and unverified access. Sol's “larger primary” has the same upper allocation dimensions as peers. | Exterior scope can defer finishes/furniture; it cannot establish six usable suites from gross area. Which interior boundaries are necessary for an exterior product is a product/design decision to settle explicitly. |
| Generic composition is already authored | Both select hall-and-mirrored-wings. Older Azure operations reproduce the selected simple 776 m² campus and its weak rear elevation. Sol does select a roof profile, screens, portal and hollow coping. | The vocabulary permits more than boxes. There is no evidence that construction/rendering erased a richer paired proposal; neither reached construction. The older renders confirm simple chosen geometry. |

Confirmed contract pressure: the planner receives the operations-oriented system before appended planning instructions, must commit world centres, sizes, profiles, materials and openings, and cannot change principal anchors later. Exterior instructions defer fit-out while design-first instructions require individual suite/access commitments. These are competing scope pressures, not proof of private model deliberation. Retrieval already supplied estate, climate and ambition guidance. Both responses completed normally below the 30,000-token cap; truncation is not the demonstrated cause.

**Hypotheses, not findings:** duplicated coordinate work may consume attention better spent on composition; immutable early solids may encourage conservative forms; cheap/default helpers may make repeated arrangements attractive; higher-capability reasoning may help integrate section and programme. One paired sample cannot identify causal weights, infer hidden reasoning, establish general model superiority or show that relationship-first design improves aesthetics.

## Correct division of responsibility

| Decision | Model/design intelligence | Software coordination |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial organization | Select public/private/service hierarchy, suite/bath relationships, distributed versus unified estate, privacy and view priorities. | Maintain typed space/boundary/level references and explicit graph connectivity; report missing or contradictory obligations. |
| Form | Select outlines, curves, mass proportions, roof sections, voids, style, climate response and facade rhythm. | Preserve selected profiles and transforms; derive declared offsets/datums and evaluate actual geometry where supported. Never substitute a rectangle for a curve. |
| Door | Choose which spaces connect, host boundary, position or permitted interval, width/height and access intent. | Use one wall-local opening definition to construct matching cut and door. Check fit and datum; reject ambiguous/missing shared boundaries. |
| Stair | Choose type, location, direction, width, levels, landings and void intent. | Expand explicitly selected parameters through the existing straight-stair helper; compute risers/treads/level endpoint and check selected surfaces. A stair box is not that intent. |
| Routes | Choose corridor versus outdoor path, route sequence, thresholds and privacy constraints. | Check surface continuity, obstruction and vertical transitions for the supported geometry. Return unverified for unsupported cases. Do not add a bridge or enter another suite to satisfy a graph. |
| Roof/terrace relationship | Choose cover, separation, junction strategy, shade extent and silhouette. | Compute actual offsets and clearances including renderer extents. A conflict needs an architectural choice when no permitted adjustment is declared. |

The model is currently assigned too much repeated exact placement. Software is also being asked to trust too much narrative evidence. The right boundary is explicit selected design intent followed by deterministic expansion and honest unresolved results. Removing all coordinates would be equally wrong: adjacency alone cannot select a beautiful or even feasible embedding.

## Relationship-first planning: suitable with geometry, not a graph alone

Represent suite-to-bath and suite-to-corridor intent as different typed connections, with stable space IDs, level IDs, selected boundary IDs and an opening location/interval. Distinguish internal threshold, exterior route and vertical connection. Current `relationships` points to component anchors; current `routes` points to spaces through anchors. Neither records a shared room boundary, selected host opening dependency, or an explicit stair/landing/void system. A broad `connected-to` bounds test is not a navigability proof. `straightWalkingAccess` proves only a straight, level 0.6 m-wide/2 m-high route on parallel rectangular support surfaces; it is not whole-building circulation.

A graph can require an ensuite to belong to a suite and keep public routes out of private rooms. It cannot derive unique wall positions from those facts. Geometry must still specify the shared edge and enough sizing/position intent. For nonadjacent spaces, the designer must choose an intermediary corridor/link or change the arrangement. For several eligible boundaries, ask for selection rather than arbitrarily choosing one. For curved/profiled boundaries, preserve intent and report unsupported derivation until actual-surface handling exists. Do not outlaw the design because the rectangle proof cannot certify it.

The existing software can already expand a selected doorway, facade bay, straight stair, residential wing or rectangular pool; resolve nested rotations; place on support; retain custom arc/extrusion geometry; and check several physical relationships. It cannot currently solve room packing, general boundary loops, arbitrary route navigation, full stair headroom, terrain-aware siting or coordinated roof joints. Reusing helpers does not mean those missing responsibilities already exist.

## Smallest viable proposed change — not implemented

Keep the current Generate route, operation engine, editable scene, atomic execution and preservation gates. Introduce a **bounded revision of the existing proposal contract**, subject to the independent audit:

1. In the design response, separate selected architectural geometry from non-solid spatial reservations. Use existing space/anchor IDs plus explicit boundary/opening and level/stair dependencies. Mark which selected dimensions are fixed, which offsets are derived and what adjustments the designer permits. Do not treat a reservation as renderable committed construction.
2. Before freezing anchors, resolve only explicit, unambiguous dependencies using existing doorway/facade/stair expansions and shared transforms. Materialize generated IDs and a provenance ledger from dependency to parts. A missing boundary, insufficient stair parameters or incompatible constraints returns unresolved; no invented architectural decisions or silent resize.
3. Validate the materialized proposal with existing checks. Return concrete conflicts to the design stage or stop for review; do not add an automatic paid retry. Freeze the resolved geometry, then use the existing construction response for detail and existing fidelity review to preserve it.
4. Keep arbitrary direct parts and custom profiles. If an unfamiliar shape cannot use the bounded resolver, require explicit geometry or an unresolved finding; never coerce it into a house template. Test movement/edit invalidation of dependent parts before product integration.

An illustrative intent (a proposal for contract review, **not** accepted API JSON) is: `connect suite-a → gallery-b; host boundary-west; local opening centre=2.1 m; width=1.1 m; height=2.3 m; sill=level-one`. Its cut and panel should not each receive separately guessed world coordinates. A stair needs more: selected straight configuration, two level references, origin, heading, width, run/risers and explicit landing/void references. “Provide stairs” is insufficient.

This change touches proposal representation, dependency materialization and fidelity semantics, so it is a substantial architectural decision and was not implemented. The offline experiment is a bounded proof of arithmetic only. Before approval, specify ID collision policy, provenance retention, dependency-cycle errors, edit invalidation, reservation-versus-solid migration and legacy-proposal readability. Resolve dependencies in order: selected levels/frames → selected boundaries → openings/infill; selected levels/stair parameters → steps/landings/void checks → route evidence; selected mass/roof intent → section conflicts. Cycles or conflicting fixed commitments return to the designer.

Alternatives: richer instructions alone cost little but both outputs already demonstrate their limits; a stronger model under the frozen contract isolates model capability but retains duplicated coordination; a complete constrained-layout solver could solve more embeddings but is a large new design system with style/feasibility risks; template placement is easy to validate but sacrifices the required freedom. Prefer the bounded proposal revision if independent review supports it.

## Offline generalization and narrow correction

Run `npx vite-node --config vitest.config.mts scripts/architectural-planning-audit-offline.ts`. It imports existing helpers and schema adapters, denies fetch and never invokes Generate. [Results](offline-results.json) contain all fixture inputs, resulting parts and assertions.

| Deterministic fixture | Different arrangement/relationship | Result and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Compact family | 5 × 7 m suite; ensuite enters suite; suite enters side corridor; world shift (7, −13). | Two explicit shared-edge thresholds retain matching opening/panel centres and walking sill. Not a completed room plan. |
| Distributed luxury estate | 11 × 14 m suite group in a rotated frame; separate offset pavilion decks with an explicitly selected outdoor link; unrelated curved screen. | Threshold transforms match; link contacts both decks at a common datum; decks remain separated without it; arc geometry retained. Contact alone does not certify clear, sheltered full-route access. |
| Constrained multilevel | 3.2 × 8.5 m upper suite at Y=3.4; suite and bath separately enter corridor; different rotation/IDs. Selected stair rises 3.2 m in 16 steps over 4.8 m. | Thresholds align; last tread reaches upper datum exactly; bad stair ratio refused. Landings, opening and headroom remain explicit unresolved design obligations. |

Four negative cases refuse missing opening location, detached boundaries, corner-only contact and an opening outside the shared-edge margin. No opening is invented. The experiment handles chosen local straight edges, not general polygon/curve intersection or automatic room arrangement. Retaining an arc elsewhere proves non-coercion, not curved-threshold placement. These tests establish general arithmetic reuse across sizes/frames/topologies, not architectural creativity, complete house feasibility or model quality.

**Implemented correction:** proposal `relationships` accepts up to 80, while `spatialCoordinationFindings` parsed at most 40. An otherwise accepted 41–80-entry declaration produced only a generic contract finding, masking detailed review. The reviewer now accepts the proposal budget. Regression checks 80 valid declarations, a contradiction in entry 80, and rejection at 81. Existing construction intent remains capped at 40; no provider budget, physical tolerance or acceptance requirement was relaxed. The paired failures have fewer than 40 relationships, so this defect did not cause them.

Verification details and exact test totals are recorded in [verification.json](verification.json). Tests cover existing proposal fidelity, route orchestration, walking evidence, assembly evidence and spatial review. Typecheck and focused lint were run. No aesthetic validator, production resolver or replacement engine was added.

## Independent audit and next experiment

Give the external reviewer [AUDIT-PROMPT.md](AUDIT-PROMPT.md) and the entire `independent-audit-package.zip`. Ask them to read raw evidence before this report. No review has been submitted or scheduled. The package separates raw paired evidence, older construction screenshots, current source and our recommendations. It includes a machine-checkable SHA-256 manifest and the full original brief.

First obtain a high-capability model's independent architectural-method review, preferably with architecture expertise or subsequent architect review. Model identity, current availability and pricing should be verified at authorization time; this audit makes no claim about the best current commercial model. A fixed review budget and no tools/automatic retries would make that experiment bounded. Historical planning estimates are approximately $0.11 Terra before cache-write premium and $0.459 Sol including the recorded premium; they are not quotes for a new model or the larger audit package.

After independent review, test **one variable at a time**: first the frozen method with a stronger planning model if the reviewer recommends it; or the same model under a reviewed relationship/dependency contract against its current contract. Use identical briefs, knowledge and output allowance, retain failures, and do not combine a model change with a method change in the first comparison. Start with the original mansion diagnostic; then compact family and constrained multilevel briefs with held-out dimensions and arrangements. Three paired repetitions per brief (18 planning responses) would be a small pilot, not a reliability certification. Construct only proposals with resolved architectural obligations, within a separately approved construction-call budget. Stop rather than spend on known invalid designs.

Before spending, set a measured input estimate including the strict schema and reserve the maximum output and any documented cache-write premium. Cost formula: `input_tokens × input_rate / 1e6 + output_cap × output_rate / 1e6`, adjusting separately for cached reads/writes under the selected provider's current tariff. Include construction, image inputs and independent visual review separately. Freeze price evidence and a total dollar ceiling; do not extrapolate the paired planning costs to two-stage house generation.

Predeclare observable outcomes: every requested suite has its intended private boundary and route; every upper occupied area has an actual selected stair/landing/opening system with surface/headroom evidence; intended dry links are continuous and unobstructed; selected forms/materials survive construction/edit replay. Classify unresolved geometry separately from a physical failure. Review unchanged standard renders with blinded human comparison for composition, entrance, climate response, facade depth, landscape and brief identity. Track valid complete proposals per attempt, human redesign needed, editable fidelity, latency and total paid cost. Keep aesthetics separate from mechanical pass rate. Do not reward area, number of parts or fewer findings as beauty.

The evidence supports reducing independent coordinate commitments and making topology explicit. It does **not** establish that a relationship graph designs beautiful architecture, that any tested model can reliably deliver a complete mansion, or that the system now produces better homes.
