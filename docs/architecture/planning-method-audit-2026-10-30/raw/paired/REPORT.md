## Decisive physical evidence

**Access and suite enclosure.** Ground access walls are centred at X=±10, Z=6, yaw=π/2, with opening local X=-2. The existing world transform places the openings at **Z=8**, while both door anchors are at **Z=4**. These are real doors at solid wall portions, not usable thresholds. Ground suite boxes/end walls terminate at X=±13, while their inner access walls sit at ±10: an approximately 3 m unclosed strip. Upper suite boxes/end walls terminate at ±13.5 while inner walls sit at ±11.75, leaving approximately 1.75 m unclosed strips. This is more developed than Terra's solid shells but still does not bound six independent suites.

The bridge spans X=-10..10 and Z=-5..-1. Northern upper doors sit at X=±11.75, Z=-5; southern doors at Z=5. The bridge alone does not reach them, but contiguous wing slabs extend to X=±10 and provide possible side-corridor surfaces. Therefore **do not interpret the missing explicit corridor route anchors as proof that every upper door is physically unreachable**. Independent private enclosure and the stair remain unresolved regardless.

**Levels and stairs.** Ground/upper walking datums are consistently stated as 0.2/4.2 m. The four upper suite box bottoms match 4.2 m. Ground suite/great-hall bottoms are 0.1 m, 0.1 m below the slab top; gallery/outdoor boxes also misuse their centre datums. The stair reservation occupies X=-1.3..1.3, Y=0.2..4.2, Z=-10.25..-3.75. It intersects the principal doorway at Z=-9. Its flat solid top cannot become 21 risers without replacing its immutable geometry; no actual stair profile or tread/landing sequence is selected. This reproduces Terra's deferred-stair failure in a more concrete, obstructive form.

**Roof geometry.** The central extrusion is a valid thin rising plane, not a generic filled roof box. Across Z=-10..10 its underside rises from Y=6.6 to 7.5 and upper surface from 6.9 to 7.8. Over the bridge Z=-5..-1, underside clearance above the 4.2 m walking datum is approximately **2.625–2.805 m**: the claimed 2.3 m bridge headroom is plausible for the roof alone. It is retained as a profile-review limitation by the existing validator, not declared a failure solely because bounds cannot certify it.

However, the roof extends X=-11..11, while wing roof bounds begin at |X|=8.8, leaving **2.2 m of plan overlap on each side**. Unlike Terra's separated-height roof overlap, the central sloped solid also reaches the wing roof's occupied vertical band over part of this junction. No cut, separation or coordinated joining profile is selected. Ground and upper wing enclosures also have gaps between their end walls and inner walls. These physical commitments need alteration, not merely more construction detail.

**Outdoor continuity and small validator findings.** Hall floor ends Z=9; terrace starts Z=11. Arrival court ends Z=-12; main entrance is at -9. These are substantive missing dry links. Arrival-spine/pool separation measures 46 m against a declared 48 m; pergola underside clearance is 3.195 m against 3.27 m. The pool-facing sign is reversed, as in Terra. Two declared relationships cite `suite-1`, `suite-2` and `ocean-terrace-space`, which are space IDs, not anchors. These relationship/reference defects are distinguished from design quality.

The coping profile opening is **23.998 × 8 m**, around a **24 × 8 m** water surface. The unchanged water-obstruction check flags the approximately 1 mm overlap at each width edge. This is a retained rounding/clearance defect, **not** a repeat of Terra's entire filled coping plate, nor a decisive reason to reject the mansion architecturally. Accurate infinity hydraulics, site slope and engineering are capability limitations; selected dry-route gaps and blocked doors are avoidable planning errors.

## Validation, preservation and decision

Current proposal validation has the previously documented slab-as-overhead correction; baseline roof-only kind mismatches are reported separately for both candidates. Sol still fails the original criteria and semantic gate independently. Full checks expose details beyond the first stopping error: Sol's gallery access includes a volume rather than only entrance kinds; its great-hall enclosure list includes glazing where enclosure references are restricted; ground suite routes contain doors without required walking-surface anchors; relationship endpoints include missing anchor references. These are recorded from exact output, not exemptions or repairs.

Sol's failure is **similar in core cause, materially different in detail**. Both choose a central social pavilion with mirrored upper wings and substitute narrative assurance for coordinated access and immutable geometry. Sol improves the facade and pool representation, gives every suite a door, and resolves four upper space datums. It also commits a blocking stair box, incorrect ground door placement, incomplete wall loops and intersecting roof junctions. It is a more detailed incomplete proposal, not a validated route to a premium mansion.
