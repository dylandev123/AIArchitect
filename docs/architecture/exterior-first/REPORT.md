# Exterior-first Generate

Normal Generate now prioritizes architectural composition and a developed exterior before interior detailing. This is an instruction, construction-contract and review change within the existing single-response direct-scene workflow. **No fresh model generation was made, so improved Terra exterior quality is not yet demonstrated.** Existing uncommitted work was preserved.

1. **Can this engine express a Solana-quality exterior?** It has much of the necessary vocabulary: independent floor and roof sections, openings, framed glazing, stone/wood finishes, portals, screens, supports, terraces and pergola members. Read `src/components/scene/architecture.tsx`, including `TropicalVilla`, `UpperSuite`, `Entrance`, `Roof`, `Glazing` and `Slats`, and inspected retained Solana and rejected-estate renders. Solana achieves depth through differentiated heights, recessed upper glazing, separate sill/header pieces, a tall stone arrival sequence and thin shaded roof layers. These principles transfer; its layout and dimensions were not copied. Exact visual parity is unproven: its arbitrary pitch/roll, decorative shapes, lighting accents and furnished interiors exceed some direct-scene capabilities. The new studies demonstrate editable construction relationships, not an equally accomplished architectural design.

2. **What prevented exterior priority?** Actual provider instructions said to construct rooms/circulation, suite subdivisions, bathroom commitments and physical kitchen fit-out, reserving budget for rooms before satellites. Programme review then treated absent interior construction as missing evidence. These priorities competed with exterior development. The retained estate nevertheless used only 128 elements: its repeated shells and blank arrival walls were authored choices, not evidence that interior work exhausted the budget. Roof, water crossing, railing and stair faults also originated in authored coordinates. No missing renderer was withholding finished architecture.

3. **What construction problems changed?** `facade-bay` is an explicitly selected existing `assemble` helper: wall-base origin, real opening, separately editable glazing/door panel, shared sill/head datums and stable `id`/`id-panel`. It rejects an opening outside its wall and raised-sill entrance selections. Rotated and nested frames are tested. It does not overlay-cut an existing shell: callers must replace/remove the uncut wall. This makes the documented fitted-opening relationship executable without selecting a facade layout. A genuine pool-helper defect was also corrected: side coping extended 0.10 m into the dry decks. Coping now abuts those decks; basin/water dimensions, outer footprint, 14-part count and metre-wide dry circuit are preserved. Existing scene files are not regenerated. Replaying retained authored operations changes only the two coping pieces; the other 126 match exactly and the full estate remains rejected. Historical files/responses were not edited.

4. **Is normal Generate genuinely exterior-first?** Yes at the provider-facing contract: concept → massing → levels → roofs → facades → entrances → terraces/pool → exterior detail. It prioritizes a developed main residence, differentiated volumes, setbacks, roof hierarchy, shading, facade depth, material contrast and coherent arrival over small objects. `constructionScope` defaults to `exterior` in the Generate provider schema; explicitly requested interior construction can select `interior-inclusive`. The real route/SDK wire test verifies these instructions reach the provider. Scope is metadata, not a beauty certificate. No model, retry, timeout, token/operation cap, renderer or second generation pipeline changed.

5. **Are interiors deferred appropriately?** Exterior programme entries allocate actual envelope capacity to wings/levels, retaining required-brief provenance and bedroom-driven scale without claiming individually completed rooms. Missing partitions, bathroom provision, cabinets and shared room-floor allocations no longer count as exterior fit-out failures. Disclosures remain visible under deferred interiors; missing/wrong element references, unsupported provenance, exterior access, enclosure and roof evidence still produce findings. Outdoor limitations remain findings. The studio displays **“Exterior-designed, interiors unfinished.”** Historical scope stays unrecorded rather than being retroactively relabelled. Every programme space still has `verifiedComplete:false`. All previous interior helpers remain available, and interior-inclusive/historical reviews retain fit-out findings. Legacy runtime compatibility without a programme remains; this is not comprehensive brief extraction or mandatory accommodation verification.

6. **Are roofs, facades, entrances, pools and levels coordinated?** Specific offline constructions are coordinated, not all future generations. Programme overhead review now accepts the union of split roofs/overhead slabs in a shared yaw frame instead of requiring one giant roof. Rectangle subtraction retains uncovered-gap detection; mixed-yaw collective coverage remains conservatively unverified. This is evidence review, not automatic roof construction. The studies pass existing support, occupied-roof, water-obstruction, railing and circulation reviews. Existing pitched-wing roof surface/fascia checks from prior work are preserved, as are genuine retained-estate failures. Roof unions, complete access/headroom and arbitrary primitive-roof intersections are not comprehensively solved or certified. No geometric safety check was disabled.

7. **What remains unsupported?** Arbitrary mesh/CSG or polygonal solids, bespoke curves beyond the pinned pavilion, roof forms beyond flat/gable/hip, automatic roof junctions, arbitrary pitch/roll, automatic whole-house layout/access planning, specialist hydraulic systems, generation-time sloped-site construction and structural/code certification. `pavilion` remains an opaque composite; the new studies use independently editable primitives instead. Landscaping/site integration remains subject to existing grounds capabilities and flat Generate staging; no new planting/terrain authoring system was added.

8. **Can it support substantially different designs/value levels?** The contracts impose no house dimensions, style, footprint, amenity list or value-to-area formula. Existing property-value versus construction-budget separation is preserved. Instructions retain bedroom capacity, levels, site/climate assumptions, privacy, views and requested exterior amenities. The compact gable and stepped flat-roof studies demonstrate different scales and organizations. They are not $200k/$25m price validations, production templates or proof of reliable diversity across prompts.

9. **What is actually proven offline?** The compact study has 21 editable elements; the layered study has 98, including a recessed upper envelope, separate roof sections, fitted facade bays, balcony, supported exterior stair/landing, canopy, pergola and connected pool circuit. Both have zero current architectural-review issues and zero relationship findings; neither is a completed residence. Unsupported disclosures still identify unfinished stair guards and unverified full route/headroom, engineering and interior work. JSON reload, panel edits and real project-store Undo/Redo are tested. Eight local renders verify exact rendered element IDs/transforms and no browser errors/context loss. API requests (including `/api/assets`) and external page resources were blocked during capture. Passing checks does not establish beauty or construction readiness.

10. **What needs one live Terra generation?** Whether Terra follows the new priorities, preserves every brief requirement and selects varied massing, resolves all roof/access/site coordinates, spends its budget on convincing exterior detail and produces a sophisticated first-request result. One fresh response plus arrival, garden, aerial and poolside review would be an initial check, not evidence of reliability across all climates, styles and property-value levels. No live call was made or scheduled.
