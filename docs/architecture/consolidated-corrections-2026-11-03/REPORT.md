## 7. Does the standard Generate workflow preserve rejected plans?

Yes, on the real route. A `DesignConflict` after a parsed proposal returns 422 with `{error, providerCalls, decisions, rejected}`. `rejected` = `{status:'rejected', completedHouse:false, scene:null, persisted:false, stage, notReached, compilerRejection, plan (as authored), designDecisions, limitations, diagnostics, measured, coordination, corrections, concept, drawing (SVG labelled NOT A BUILDING), notes}`. Bounded: 300 findings, 400 k-char drawing, 900 k-char total (shed in a stated order); unreadable provider output is kept as received (≤60 k chars). Never a scene, never saved, never applied to a project. The studio shows it in `RejectedPlanPanel` (reasons, findings, offers, drawing) with JSON/SVG export and Dismiss. The route performs no authorization of its own (any access control is the app's proxy/middleware, which I did not change or verify); the response goes only to the requesting client and nothing is logged or stored. **No production database change and no migration** were needed or made; durable storage of rejected plans would need one and is not proposed here.

## 8. Are knowledge and programme more faithful to the brief?

* **Was the full brief in the programme?** The user message *was* the whole brief. What was missing is any check of it: `deriveBriefProgramme`/`checkBriefProgramme` existed only for revisions. They now run on Generate (and on rejected plans) as report-only findings.
* **Six suites without bathrooms:** the brief says "approximately six generous bedroom suites"; the plan has six bedroom spaces and **no bathroom space**, so 0 suites are established (need 5–7). Reported with the brief's own phrase. The instructions now say what a suite is (a bedroom with a bathroom space joined by a door, or stated facilities) without asking for any size, storey count or complexity.
* **Daylight:** the two salons have doors to outdoor spaces but no window. Door glazing is not modelled, so this is "daylight unestablished" (subjective finding), not "dark" and not an error.
* **Six identical 7×5 m cells:** reported as a design observation for a person (the mission already says to avoid repetitive suites); no score, no threshold gate.
* **Entrance:** the revision gate counted interior rooms only, so an outdoor arrival porch looked like a missing entrance. For Generate the arrival space counts.
* **Parser fix:** "two bedroom wings" was read as "two bedrooms".
* **Strategy text:** `strategy-waterfront-central-pavilion` told the model to join wings to "the pavilion flanks" without saying what that means for a pavilion with a wide terrace; it now states the `attached` declaration with a worked example, and that each suite has its own bath space. Field limits (700 chars) respected.
* **Composition fields vs claim:** composition carried volumes, roofs, facades and entrance; the weaknesses were not missing fields but placement arithmetic. The instruction addendum (appended after the frozen construction paragraphs, which remain verbatim) covers wing extents, shared-wall openings, site/landscape and programme fidelity.

## 10. Honest limits and what still prevents reliable model-authored construction

* **RC1 relies on the model declaring `attached`** (or anchoring to the room it is entered from). The instructions and strategy now teach it; whether Terra does so is untested — deliberately, no paid call was made.
* **RC3 changes a historical contract on purpose.** Explicit-side openings are no longer literal where the literal position missed the shared wall. Retained-evidence expectations moved accordingly and are documented in the tests: the original saved mansion's diagnosis is 53 findings (was 57; four explicit-side doors are now fitted), two coordination tests and one alignment-direction test were rewritten. Plans that already compiled are byte-identical; `savedProjects.test.ts` confirms.
* **RC4:** the site remains a model-authored number. No request path currently provides a fixed client boundary, so `siteBasis` defaults to `model-defined`; the derived minimum is an offer. Whether to let software *derive* a fictional site is a product decision, not taken here.
* `corrections.ts` classifies; it cannot make a plan that has other unresolved defects "verified". By design, on the retained plan nothing is.
* Interior-connection loss is measured as shared walls wide enough for a door; it is not a circulation proof.
* `diagnosePlan` reports facade-bay fit through the shared opening checks; span height equality and span cover are still enforced only by the compiler.
* Not changed (out of scope, noted): a legacy-recipe record can still reach a canonical request; pitched-roof, curved, planting, grading and real water-edge geometry remain unsupported; subjective qualities (proportion, silhouette, material richness, arrival drama) are not measurable and are not scored.
* Developer scripts: five earlier one-off live-call scripts under `scripts/` do not type-check (`canonical-acceptance-{diagnose,once,review}.ts`, `canonical-final-{once,review}.ts`; 12 errors, present before this work; `src/` is clean). They make `npm run build` and `npm run typecheck` fail as the repository stands.
